# Business Terms

## Purpose

This document defines standardized terminology related to business analysis, software requirements, and business processes.

Its purpose is to establish a shared vocabulary across the QA-AI framework, ensuring that business-related documentation, skills, workflows, templates, and testing artifacts use consistent terminology.

---

## A

### Acceptance Criteria

**Definition**

Conditions that must be satisfied before a requirement, user story, or feature is considered complete and accepted.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- User Story
- Validation

---

### Actor

**Definition**

A person, system, or external entity that interacts with a software system to achieve a specific objective.

**Related Terms**

- User
- Role
- Business Process

---

## B

### Business Goal

**Definition**

A desired business outcome that a solution or initiative is intended to achieve.

**Related Terms**

- Business Objective
- Requirement

---

### Business Objective

**Definition**

A measurable business outcome that guides the implementation of a feature, product, or process.

**Related Terms**

- Business Goal
- Requirement

---

### Business Process

**Definition**

A sequence of business activities performed to achieve a specific business objective.

**Related Terms**

- Workflow
- Actor
- Business Rule

---

### Business Rule

**Definition**

A statement that defines or constrains business behavior, decisions, or operations.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- Validation Rule
- Constraint

---

## C

### Constraint

**Definition**

A limitation or restriction that affects how a business process or system may operate.

**Related Terms**

- Business Rule
- Requirement

---

## D

### Dependency

**Definition**

A relationship in which one requirement, process, or feature relies on another.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- Business Process

---

## F

### Functional Requirement

**Definition**

A requirement that specifies what the system must do.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- Non-Functional Requirement

---

## N

### Non-Functional Requirement (NFR)

**Definition**

A requirement that specifies quality attributes or operational characteristics of a system rather than its functional behavior.

**Related Terms**

- Functional Requirement
- Performance
- Security

---

## R

### Requirement

**Definition**

A documented need, expectation, or capability that a system must satisfy.

**Related Terms**

- Functional Requirement
- Non-Functional Requirement
- Acceptance Criteria

---

## S

### Stakeholder

**Definition**

An individual, group, or organization that has an interest in or is affected by a system, project, or business process.

**Related Terms**

- Actor
- User

---

### Scope

**Definition**

The defined boundaries of work, functionality, or business processes included within an initiative.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- Business Process

---

## U

### Use Case

**Definition**

A description of interactions between actors and a system to achieve a specific business objective.

**Related Terms**

- Actor
- Business Process
- User Story

---

### User

**Definition**

An individual who interacts directly with a software system.

**Related Terms**

- Actor
- Stakeholder

---

### User Story

**Definition**

A concise description of a feature from the perspective of an end user, expressing who needs the functionality, what is needed, and why.

**Related Terms**

- Requirement
- Acceptance Criteria
- Use Case

---

## V

### Validation Rule

**Definition**

A rule that defines the conditions under which data or business operations are considered valid.

**Related Terms**

- Business Rule
- Constraint
- Acceptance Criteria

---

## W

### Workflow

**Definition**

A defined sequence of activities performed to complete a business process or achieve a business objective.

**Related Terms**

- Business Process
- Use Case
- Actor
