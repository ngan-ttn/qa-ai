# Review Pattern

## Purpose

The `Review` pattern defines a reusable reasoning approach for evaluating the quality of existing artifacts.

Its purpose is to identify strengths, weaknesses, improvement opportunities, and review findings while preserving objective and evidence-based reasoning.

This pattern focuses on **quality evaluation** rather than validation or artifact generation.

---

## When To Use

Apply this pattern whenever AI needs to evaluate the quality of an existing artifact.

Typical use cases include:

- Requirement review
- Scenario review
- Test case review
- Bug report review
- Regression review
- Documentation review

---

## Core Principles

An effective review should:

- Evaluate against defined objectives.
- Remain objective and evidence-based.
- Distinguish observations from recommendations.
- Identify both strengths and weaknesses.
- Produce actionable review findings.

---

## Review Process

A complete review typically follows these activities.

### 1. Understand the Review Context

Determine what is being reviewed and why.

Typical considerations include:

- Artifact type
- Review objectives
- Review criteria
- Intended audience

---

### 2. Evaluate Quality

Assess the artifact against relevant quality expectations.

Typical considerations include:

- Completeness
- Correctness
- Consistency
- Clarity
- Maintainability

---

### 3. Identify Findings

Identify observations discovered during the review.

Typical considerations include:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Risks
- Gaps
- Improvement opportunities

---

### 4. Assess Impact

Determine the significance of identified findings.

Typical considerations include:

- Business impact
- Testing impact
- Maintainability impact
- Downstream impact

---

### 5. Produce Review Results

Organize findings into a structured review outcome suitable for downstream decision making.

---

## Expected Outcome

A successful review should produce:

- Objective quality assessment
- Clearly identified findings
- Improvement opportunities
- Review recommendations
- Structured review results

---

## Design Guidelines

The review pattern should:

- Remain domain-independent.
- Focus on evidence-based evaluation.
- Avoid unsupported opinions.
- Distinguish observations from recommendations.
- Support informed decision making.

---

## Relationships

This pattern is commonly used by:
