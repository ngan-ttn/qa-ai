# Government

> Version: 1.0.0  
> Status: Approved  
> Last Updated: 2026-08-13

## Overview

**Government** systems support public services, applications, permits, licensing, benefits, case management, records, payments, and regulated administrative decisions.

## Purpose

Provide reusable QA context for rule-heavy, auditable public-sector workflows without assuming a jurisdiction's law or procedure.

## Core Concepts

### Applicant and Authority
Service users interact with authorized public bodies and roles.
### Application/Case
A tracked request or administrative matter.
### Eligibility and Evidence
Decisions can depend on formal criteria and documents.
### Decision and Appeal
Outcomes may require reason, notice, review, or appeal paths.
### Public Record and Privacy
Transparency and confidentiality obligations can coexist.

## How It Works

Applications are submitted, validated, assessed, decided, communicated, and retained under defined authority and policy.

## When to Use

Use for permits, licensing, benefits, public portals, case management, and government payments.

## When Not to Use

Do not infer legal deadlines, eligibility, retention, or disclosure rules.

## Advantages

Highlights authorization, fairness, traceability, accessibility, and policy-version risks.

## Limitations

Requirements vary by agency and jurisdiction.

## Examples

A permit decision may depend on application completeness, approval authority, validity period, and auditable reasons for rejection.

## Best Practices

- Trace decisions to approved rules.
- Verify role authority and audit trail.
- Test effective dates and policy versions.
- Cover accessibility and privacy requirements when specified.
