# Regulatory Requirements

> Version: 1.0.0  
> Status: Approved  
> Last Updated: 2026-08-13

## Overview

**Regulatory requirements** are externally imposed obligations from applicable laws, regulations, regulators, licenses, or binding rules that influence business and system behavior.

## Purpose

Help QA recognize regulatory impact while requiring authoritative, jurisdiction-specific evidence before asserting obligations.

## Core Concepts

### Authority
The body or legal source imposing an obligation.
### Applicability
Jurisdiction, entity, product, data, user, and effective date determine scope.
### Obligation
Required or prohibited behavior.
### Evidence
Records demonstrating compliance.
### Change
Rules can be amended, replaced, or become effective later.

## How It Works

Applicable obligations are interpreted by authorized business/legal owners, translated into product controls and evidence, then verified through requirements and tests.

## When to Use

Use for regulated products, privacy, financial services, healthcare, government, retention, reporting, and audit requirements.

## When Not to Use

Do not provide legal conclusions or infer applicability from generic industry knowledge.

## Advantages

Regulatory awareness helps identify high-impact omissions and evidence needs.

## Limitations

Interpretation and applicability require qualified authoritative sources.

## Examples

A requirement may mandate retention of specific records for a defined period; QA validates the approved system requirement, not a guessed statutory period.

## Best Practices

- Record jurisdiction and authoritative source.
- Trace obligations to product requirements.
- Verify effective dates and scope.
- Preserve evidence of tested controls.
- Escalate ambiguous legal interpretation.
